[42] decision decision://hash-algo
We use sha256 in src/hash.py — collision rate is acceptable for our scale and the FIPS-compliance check passes. The block-cipher mode (BLAKE2b) was rejected because of the standardization gap for embedded targets, even though it's ~2× faster on x86-64. If we ever ship to a constrained device, revisit; otherwise sha256 is the long-term choice. Captured from src/hash.py:1-12 at commit f3bf1d4 (branch=main) on 2026-05-28.
